How to play

Build a soccer team out of Pokémon. Pick who plays where, then see how they do over a full season.

Modes

▸ 38–0 ChallengeDraft a squad from random rolls, then try to win all 38 league games.
🌍 Region LeagueDraft mostly from your home region. Finish top 4 to reach Victory Road against the other regions.
⚽ XI BuilderFree play. Build any lineup you want and share it. No games are played.

The pitch

  • Your team attacks up the pitch: keeper at the bottom, strikers at the top.
  • The label under each player is their position. Colours show the line: GK keeper, DEF defence, MID midfield, FWD attack.
  • Hover (or long-press) a player to see their base stats.

Cards also show the total of all six stats in the top-left corner. Colours: 110+ elite, 80+ good, 55+ average.

A sandbox for building and sharing your dream lineup.

  • Tap a player to open the picker. Search by name, Dex number or type (e.g. "dragon"), and sort by any stat. Press Enter to take the first result.
  • Drag one player onto another to swap them. Picking someone already in your squad also swaps them.
  • Formation moves your players into a new shape. Bench adds 5 substitutes.
  • Generation limits the pool to one region (or all of them). Legendaries lets them in.
  • ★ Best XI fills the pitch with a strong default lineup. 🔀 Random rolls a random one.
  • Undo / Redo (⌘Z / ⇧⌘Z) step through your changes.
  • ⤓ Download image saves a picture of your lineup. 🔗 Link copies a link that opens this exact lineup.

Draft 11 Pokémon from random rolls, then play a 38-game league. Can you go 38–0?

The draft

  1. Pick your Pokémon pool (all generations or Gen 1–4) and a formation. They're locked for the whole run.
  2. Each round rolls a region and a type. You can only pick Pokémon that match both.
  3. Tap a Pokémon, then tap a glowing open spot on the pitch to place it. Placements are final.
  4. You get 2 rerolls per run and can use up to 3 Legendaries (★).

The season

  • You play 19 clubs home and away. Each club has its own squad of Pokémon, from weak to very strong.
  • Watch it play out live or hit Skip. Win = 3 points, draw = 1.
  • Afterwards, check the Squad, Team, Table, Awards and Games tabs. Tap any club to see its squad and results.
  • Each of your players gets a performance rating for the season, based on what they actually did.

A perfect 38–0 is extremely rare. Even an elite squad pulls it off only about 1 run in 70.

📅 Daily Challenge

  • Everyone gets the same rolls and formation each day, with one attempt. A new Daily starts at midnight.
  • Share your result as an emoji grid of all 38 games and compare with friends.

⚔️ Challenge a friend

  • After a 38–0 or Region League run, tap Challenge a friend to send a link. (Dailies already have everyone on the same rolls, so just share your result.)
  • Your friend drafts from your exact rolls with the same formation and plays the same league, against the same clubs with the same luck in each fixture. Your squad stays hidden until they finish.
  • Then you both see a side-by-side comparison and a two-leg derby between your XIs. They can send the result back to you.

Represent your home region, win its league, then take on the best of the others.

  1. Choose a home region and formation.
  2. Draft as in the 38–0 Challenge, but 75% of rolls come from your home region (marked 🏠).
  3. Play a 38-game league against clubs named after towns in your region.
  4. Finish top 4 to qualify for ⛰️ Victory Road: 16 clubs from every region.

Victory Road format

  • Group stage: 4 groups of 4, home and away (6 games). Only the group winner goes through.
  • Final Four: only the four group winners go through, to semi-finals and then a final at a neutral ground. Level after 90 minutes means extra time, then penalties.
  • Victory Road clubs are stronger than most regional clubs, so expect a tougher fight.
  • Win it all to enter the 🏆 Hall of Fame.

Matches are simulated from your players' stats and where you put them. Positioning is the whole game.

What each position needs

GK HP and both defences. Bulky walls make great keepers.
CB Defence first, then HP and Sp. Def.
LB · RB · WB Defence and speed to cover the flanks.
CDM A tough shield: defence, HP, Sp. Def.
CM All-rounders. Balanced stats shine here.
LM · RM Speed above everything.
CAM Speed plus their best attacking stat.
LW · RW Very fast, with a strong attacking stat.
ST Raw attacking power (Atk or SpA), plus speed.

How a match works

  • Your forwards and midfielders make up your attack. Your defenders, keeper and midfielders make up your defence.
  • Each game compares your attack with their defence (and the reverse), then rolls the score. Better teams win more, but upsets happen.
  • Home teams get a small edge. Empty slots badly hurt your team.
  • Goals usually go to forwards, and assists to midfielders. Players in positions that suit them score and assist more.
  • Types, team chemistry and the bench don't affect results.

Season ratings

The coloured badge on each player after a season (85+ / 75+ / 65+ / below) is earned from goals, assists, clean sheets, Man of the Match awards and how well the team did.

Every season you finish is saved automatically.

  • Trophy cabinet: titles, Invincible seasons, perfect seasons and awards your players won.
  • All-time records: your best team and player seasons.
  • Pokémon careers: totals for every Pokémon that has played for you, across all runs.
  • All-Time XI: the pitch shows your best performers by position.
  • Seasons: tap any season for its awards, champions and squad stats.

Sharing & backups

  • 📤 Share result (after a season) sends your result image and summary to friends.
  • History is saved in this browser only. Use Export to back it up and Import to restore it on another device.
